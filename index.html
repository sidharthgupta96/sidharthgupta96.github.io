BUILDING IN PUBLIC
● PERSONAL SIDE PROJECT

CashRabbit.in

A cashback and affiliate rewards platform I am currently building independently — sitting between consumer purchase intent on the demand side and brand marketing / affiliate commission budgets on the supply side.

Consumer FinTech Cashback Affiliate Commerce Marketplace Growth Product Building

01. The Consumer Problem

Indian online shoppers systematically leave money on the table on everyday transactions, despite brands already allocating performance marketing budgets toward completed purchases.

Commission already exists Brands can pay affiliate commissions of 1–10%+ on completed sales as part of their marketing spend. CashRabbit's model is designed to share a portion of this existing commission with the shopper.
Value is invisible to the shopper A large portion of online shoppers purchase directly from merchants without capturing the affiliate value associated with those transactions.
Trust & delayed cashback Long confirmation windows and cancellation claw-backs can create a gap between promised savings and cashback actually received.

02. The Brand Problem

As customer acquisition costs increase, brands need channels that bring high-intent shoppers to the point of purchase while paying for actual outcomes rather than impressions or clicks alone.

Performance over traffic Affiliate-led acquisition aligns marketing spend with completed transactions rather than simply paying for exposure.
High-intent demand Cashback can create an additional reason for price-sensitive shoppers across Tier 1, Tier 2 and Tier 3 India to complete a purchase.
The attribution problem As AI shopping agents begin influencing and initiating purchases, brands will increasingly need new ways to understand attribution and performance-based commissioning.

03. The CashRabbit Model

CashRabbit aims to connect the two sides of the market:

Consumer Demand The shopper is already ready to purchase and wants better value from the transaction.
Brand Supply Brands already allocate performance marketing budgets and affiliate commissions to drive completed purchases.
Shared Surplus CashRabbit aims to return a portion of the commission already paid by brands back to the shopper.

04. Initial Product Wedge

The core mechanic is inspired by validated cashback and affiliate-commerce models such as CashKaro, ShopBack and Rakuten. Rather than attempting to compete head-on with incumbents that have spent years building merchant relationships and brand trust, CashRabbit is being approached with a deliberately narrower initial wedge.

Validated behaviour Cashback is an established consumer behaviour rather than a completely new category.
Narrow initial wedge Start focused, validate consumer behaviour, merchant economics and repeat purchase behaviour before expanding the marketplace.
Build trust first The long-term opportunity depends on creating a transparent, reliable experience from click through purchase confirmation to cashback.
