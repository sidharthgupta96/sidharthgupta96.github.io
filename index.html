PRODUCT THINKING

How I Think About Products

01

Start with the customer problem

I don't start with features. I start by understanding where the customer is struggling and why.

02

Simplify complexity

Especially in fintech, the backend can be complicated. The customer experience shouldn't have to be.

03

Measure the outcome

A feature isn't successful because it shipped. I care about adoption, conversion, drop-offs, revenue and operational impact.

04

Build with constraints

Great products are built by balancing customer needs with technology, compliance, business and operational realities.

05

Own the outcome

From problem definition and PRDs to UAT, launch and iteration, I enjoy owning the entire product lifecycle.
